STEPS

Install karne ka safe tareeka

  1. Store kholo

    Play Store ya App Store me “Free Fire MAX” search karo aur publisher verify karo.

  2. Space check karo

    Kam se kam 2 GB free rakho, warna install ke beech fail ho sakta hai.

  3. Wi-Fi pe download

    Bada download hai — Wi-Fi use karo aur background apps band rakho.

  4. Guest se login

    Pehle guest/authorised method se login karo, phir account link karo. OTP kisi ko mat do.

  5. Permissions review

    Sirf zaroori permissions do. Koi app “diamond generator” bole to turant uninstall.
