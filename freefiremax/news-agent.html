Naya agent reveal — recon + breach kit

Ek high-mobility recon pick jo squad entry se pehle vision deta hai. Kit kya karta hai, aur comp me kahan fit hota hai.

By FFMAX Editorial (fan)· 20 October 2026· 3 min read

Kit ka idea

Recon + breach combo ka matlab: pehle building ke andar ka short ping, phir ek entry window jo defender ke angle ko todta hai. Yeh rusher ko blind push nahi, planned entry deta hai.

  • Recon: ek short range scan jo sirf movement batata hai, exact position nahi.
  • Breach: ek utility jo temporary opening/cover-break create karta hai.
  • Passive: slight mobility boost after utility use — reposition ke liye.

Comp me kahan fit hota hai

Balanced comp me yeh flex slot le sakta hai — rusher ke saath recon, ya support ke saath breach. Sirf ek squad me do recon picks rakhna usually overkill hota hai.

Info pehle, entry baad me. Recon agent ka kaam fight jeetna nahi, fight ko sasta banana hai.

Solo vs squad

  • Squad: breach utility se entry, recon se second-floor clear.
  • Solo: recon se third-party detect karo, breach se escape route banao.
  • Clash Squad: economy rounds me utility value sabse zyada.

Ability numbers aur exact behaviour patch ke saath badalte hain — in-game description authoritative hai.
