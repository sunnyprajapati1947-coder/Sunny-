# नीलू और ज्ञान का चश्मा — Neelu and the Glasses of Knowledge

**Format:** 10 minutes · 36 shots · 3D CGI cartoon · Hindi voice-over + Hindi subtitles
**Genre:** moral story / friendship, in the "Hindi Kahaniya" village-story format
**Moral:** मेहनत से कमाया ज्ञान सबसे बड़ा खज़ाना है — कोई जादू उसका मुकाबला नहीं कर सकता।

---

## Characters

| | |
|---|---|
| **बिट्टू (Bittu)** | 10-year-old village boy, mustard t-shirt, blue shorts. Sharp, funny, allergic to homework. |
| **नीलू (Neelu)** | Chubby sky-blue robot cat from the 22nd century. Red scarf, star antenna, a 4-D pocket full of gadgets. Crash-landed in the jungle outside Mohanpur. |
| **माँ (Ma)** | Bittu's mother. Green saree. Wakes him up, believes in him. |
| **मास्टर जी (Master ji)** | White kurta, grey beard. Strict, then proud. |
| **ठाकुर काका (Thakur kaka)** | Village elder. Knows where the old pipe is buried. |

## Structure (three acts)

### Act I — the wish (shots 1–11, ≈2:30)
1–2 Mohanpur at sunrise; Bittu runs to school, allergic to books.
3 Ma shouts; he hides under the quilt and wishes for magic that would make him learn without studying.
4 He lies on the rooftop; a shooting star falls.
5 Crash in the jungle; the village wakes in fear.
6 He runs into the jungle with a torch.
7 **Reveal** — Neelu steps out of a cracked blue pod.
8 Neelu waves: "नमस्ते! मैं नीलू हूँ, बाईसवीं सदी से आया हुआ रोबो-बिल्ला।"
9 The 4-D pocket spills gadgets across his bedroom.
10 The one rule: a gadget that is misused stops working.
11 **The knowledge glasses** — whatever you have read once, you will remember.

### Act II — the shortcut and the price (shots 12–30, ≈5:30)
12–13 The maths exam. He wears the glasses secretly; answers pour out.
14 First position. Master ji hugs him; the class claps.
15 Celebration: sweets, dhol, Ma's tears.
16 Alone on the rooftop: *"ये जीत मेरी नहीं थी।"*
17 Neelu: *"मुझे लगा था तुम नियम का मतलब समझोगे।"*
18 The hand pump breaks; the village cracks in the heat.
19 A queue of empty buckets; children crying.
20 Thakur kaka: the old pipe is under the hillside — *"समझ चाहिए, ताकत नहीं।"*
21 The glasses say nothing: they recall what was read, they do not think.
22 The village looks at him in silence.
23 **Neelu's line** — *"असली जवाब दिमाग़ में होता है…"*
24 **The confession** — he tells the whole village he wore the glasses.
25 **Forgiveness** — *"सच बोलने की हिम्मत करने वाला बच्चा, झूठ से जीतने वाले सौ बच्चों से बेहतर है।"*
26 All-night study: pressure, siphon, lever.
27 Dawn: a plan on paper.
28 He explains the siphon on a slate.
29 The village digs, carries, lifts.
30 **Water!** Rainbow, dancing, the pump roars back to life.

### Act III — the real treasure (shots 31–36, ≈2:00)
31 Master ji: *"आज गाँव का असली हीरो ये है!"*
32 Rooftop laughter: *"असली जादू यंत्र में नहीं, तुम्हारे दिमाग़ में था।"*
33 The glasses go back into the pocket.
34 The honest exam — fewer marks, a bigger smile.
35 Farewell under the stars: *"वादा करो, हर साल गाँव आना।"*
36 Final wide + moral card.
