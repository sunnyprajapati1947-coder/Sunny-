How to play

Three taps and you are playing.

  1. 🔤 A name appears — like “Where is Bihar?”
  2. 👆 Tap that state’s shape on the map.
  3. ✅ Green means right. Red shows the answer, so you learn it.

Tip: on Easy the state names are printed on the map.
