Lyric Video Kit

Apna gaana + lyrics daalo → watercolour animated lyric video (1920×1080). Hindi aur English dono chalte hain.

🎬 Studio kholo ⏱️ Lyrics timing tool
1
Gaana daalo
Apni mp3 file ko lyricvideo/assets/song.mp3 naam se rakh do.
2
Lyrics time karo
timing tool kholo → lyrics paste karo → gaana chalao → har line pe Space dabao → "Copy" → src/song.js me paste.
3
Dekho
studio me slider se scrub karo, ya "Bake 4s preview" se chhota animation chala ke dekho.
4
MP4 banao
node render.mjs --frames=0: --workers=4 phir node render.mjs --encode (Chrome + ffmpeg chahiye)

Scenes available: sunburst · night · city · rain · typo · waves · confetti · spotlight — har line ke liye src/song.js me 4th value me scene ka naam de sakte ho.
